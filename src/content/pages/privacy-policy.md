# Privacy Policy for Toebe Consulting LLC

> Effective Date: October 31, 2025

### 1. Introduction
- Purpose: State that Toebe Consulting LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to our website (toebeconsulting.com) and clients of our services. This policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.
- Scope: Clarify that this policy applies to information collected through our website, email communications, and any services you engage us for.

### 2. Information We Collect

#### Directly Provided Information:

- Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, job title when you fill out forms (e.g., contact, scheduling, newsletter sign-up), or communicate with us directly.
- Service-Related Information: Details you provide during consultations or engagements, which may include business challenges, operational data, technical infrastructure details, and strategic objectives. (Be clear this is client data, handled under separate agreements).

#### Automatically Collected Information (Website Usage Data):

- Log Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring/exit pages, dates/timestamps of visits.
- Cookies & Tracking Technologies: Explain that we use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to enhance user experience, analyze site usage, and for marketing purposes. (Mention specific tools like Google Analytics, HubSpot's tracking code).
- Device Information: Information about the device you use to access our website (e.g., device type, unique device identifiers).

### 3. How We Use Your Information

#### To Provide and Improve Services:
- To respond to your inquiries and provide the consulting services you request.
- To operate, maintain, and improve our website and services.
- To personalize your experience on our website.

#### For Communication:

- To send you administrative information, such as updates to our terms, conditions, and policies.
- To send you marketing communications (e.g., newsletters, service updates, M&A playbooks) that we believe may be of interest to you, from which you can opt-out.

#### For Analytics and Research:
- To understand how users interact with our website and services, helping us to optimize content and user experience.
- To perform internal research and analysis.

#### For Security and Legal Compliance:

- To protect the security and integrity of our website and services.
- To comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, and enforce our terms and conditions.

### 4. How We Share Your Information

- With Service Providers: We may share your information with third-party vendors, consultants, and other service providers who perform services on our behalf (e.g., website hosting, analytics, CRM like HubSpot, email delivery, scheduling tools). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes for which it was disclosed.
- For Legal Reasons: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal processes, protect our rights or property, or ensure the safety of our users or the public.
- Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email and/or a prominent notice on our website of any change in ownership or uses of your personal information.
- With Your Consent: We may share your information with third parties when we have your explicit consent to do so.

### 5. Data Retention

- We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

### 6. Your Privacy Rights

- Access, Correction, Deletion: You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information that we hold.
- Opt-Out of Marketing: You can opt-out of receiving marketing communications from us at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions in our emails or by contacting us directly.
- Cookie Preferences: You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.
- Specific Regional Rights (e.g., GDPR, CCPA): If your target audience includes individuals in the EU or California, you must include specific sections detailing their rights under GDPR (Right to be forgotten, Right to restriction of processing, Data portability, etc.) and CCPA (Right to know, Right to delete, Right to opt-out of sale). This is where legal counsel is critical.

### 7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

- What are Cookies? Cookies are small text files stored by your browser that help websites remember your activity, preferences, or login information between visits.
- How We Use Them: Detail the types of cookies used (e.g., essential, performance, functionality, advertising) and their purpose (e.g., remembering preferences, analyzing traffic, delivering targeted ads).

### 8. Data Security
We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
9. Third-Party Links
Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.
10. Children's Privacy
Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information.
