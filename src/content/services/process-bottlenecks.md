### When Growth Stalls at the Chokepoint

Rapid growth, particularly in SaaS and SaaS-IoT, often exposes hidden inefficiencies. These aren't just minor annoyances; they are critical bottlenecks that choke innovation, delay product launches, and erode team morale. For companies undergoing M&A, these challenges are amplified, as disparate systems and cultures collide.

#### Are you experiencing:

- **Vendor Relationship Inefficiencies:** Unclear contracts, misaligned roadmaps, and poor communication with third-party vendors leading to unpredictable timelines and budget overruns.
- **Engineering-Product Communication Gaps:** Misunderstandings between engineering and product teams resulting in scope creep, rework, and missed delivery deadlines.
- **Stalled Technical Roadmaps:** Critical projects perpetually delayed due to unforeseen dependencies, resource contention, or a lack of clear process.
- **Inconsistent Delivery Across Teams:** Especially prevalent post-acquisition, where integrating teams struggle to standardize workflows, leading to fragmented output and quality issues.
- **Context Loss Post-Acquisition:** Acquired teams losing critical operational context, leading to duplicated efforts, re-inventing the wheel, and a slowdown in integration velocity.

These bottlenecks don't just slow you down; they directly impact your ability to capitalize on market opportunities and realize the full value of your investments, particularly in M&A scenarios.
